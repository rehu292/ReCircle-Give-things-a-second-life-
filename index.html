MAKE BETTER EVERYDAY CHOICES

Small habits. Lasting impact.

Simple ideas to reuse and handle everyday materials responsibly.

▤

Paper & Books

Reuse notebooks, donate readable books and keep paper dry for recycling.

TIP: Use both sides of paper.
◇

Plastic

Reuse safe containers where appropriate and check local recycling guidance.

TIP: Avoid single-use plastic.
▱

Electronics

Repair working devices, donate usable ones and use authorised e-waste collection.

TIP: Never mix e-waste with regular waste.
♧

Clothing

Repair, exchange or donate wearable clothes instead of discarding them.

TIP: Try a clothing swap.

A MORE RESPONSIBLE FUTURE

Consume thoughtfully.
Waste less.

Sustainable Development Goal 12 encourages responsible consumption and production. It reminds us to use resources wisely, reduce waste and make better choices in everyday life.

ReCircle supports this goal by helping individuals reuse, exchange, repair and donate products. When people take small actions together, they can encourage a more sustainable community.
